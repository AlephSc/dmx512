DMX512 ESP32 + MAX485 Wiring Diagram
=====================================

ESP32 (NodeMCU/DevKit)          MAX485 Module               DMX XLR-5 Connector
----------------------          -------------               -------------------
                                                            
GPIO17 (TX2) ---------------> DI (Data Input)              
                                                            
GPIO16 (RX2) <--------------- RO (Data Output)             
                                                            
GPIO21 (RTS) ---------------> DE (Driver Enable)           
              |                                             
              +-------------> RE (Receiver Enable)          
                                                            
3.3V or 5V ----------------> VCC                            
                                                            
GND -----------------------> GND ----------------------> Pin 1 (Signal Common)
                                                            
                             A (Data+) ----------------> Pin 3 (Data+)
                                                            
                             B (Data-) ----------------> Pin 2 (Data-)
                                                            
                                                         Pin 4 (Not used)
                                                         Pin 5 (Not used)


TERMINATION (Last device in chain):
====================================
120Ω resistor between Pin 2 and Pin 3 (Data- and Data+)


NOTES:
======
1. DE dan RE tied together (same control signal)
2. GPIO HIGH = Transmit mode, LOW = Receive mode
3. MAX485 lebih stabil dengan 5V supply
4. Tambahkan 100nF capacitor antara VCC dan GND MAX485
5. XLR pinout: looking at MALE pins (solder side)
6. Female XLR untuk OUTPUT, Male XLR untuk INPUT
